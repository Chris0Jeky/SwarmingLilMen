What to try
Start at Critical threshold. Move K slowly across the point where r begins to remain high.
Use Local phase waves, then reduce coupling range. Coherent domains form before global order.
Apply an External flash. The population can be temporarily entrained, then either retain or lose synchrony.
Use Chimera seed: one region starts coherent while another starts disordered under identical dynamics.
