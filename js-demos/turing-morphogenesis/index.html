Model
∂A/∂t = Dₐ∇²A − AB² + F(1−A) ∂B/∂t = Dᵦ∇²B + AB² − (K+F)B
A is replenished substrate. B consumes A and autocatalytically creates more B. B is removed faster than A is replenished. Because A and B diffuse at different rates, tiny local differences can amplify into spots, stripes, fronts and splitting domains.
