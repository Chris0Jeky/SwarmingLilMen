What to look for

No cell knows the global state. Each unstable cell only gives one grain to each orthogonal neighbour. Under slow drive and boundary dissipation, cascades span many scales: long quiet periods, small local slips, and rare system-wide avalanches.
Try Critical rain, then watch the distribution after a few hundred events. Use Dissipative fault line to see how a local sink geometry reshapes global cascade statistics.
Keyboard: Space pause, D drop, B burst, S single topple, P prime, C clear.
